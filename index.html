How to Play

  • Players take turns rolling a standard 6-sided die.
  • Pawns move automatically according to the roll value.
  • Land on the bottom of a Ladder to climb up.
  • Land on the head of a Snake to slide down.
  • Rolling a 6 awards an extra bonus turn!
  • First player to reach exactly tile 100 wins!
